Day List Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Versiunea în limba română

Day List is a personal planner for Android, provided by TMaxTech SRL ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

The content you create stays on your device

Everything you create in Day List (todos, appointments, events, reminders, shopping and wish lists, checklists, festival lineups, notes and photos) is stored only on your device. Day List has no servers and no user accounts, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of what you do in the app. Your content is never sent to us, never shared with advertisers and never sold.

Your content leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs it to, and only the part that feature needs:

  • Wish-list link previews. When you add a product link, the app loads that page directly from the website to show its title, image and price. The website receives the request like any visit from your phone.
  • Location reminders. An address you type, or your position when you choose "current location", is sent to your phone's built-in geocoding service (provided by Google on Android) to convert between an address and coordinates. Location reminders are monitored by Android on the device; your location is not sent to us.
  • Sharing and backups. Countdown share cards, lineup files and backup exports go only where you choose to send them.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google processes device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and approximate location. Ad requests contain nothing you store in the app. Google's handling of this data is described in its policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Day List asks for your consent through Google's consent form. With consent, ads may be personalized; without it, the app requests non-personalized ads only. You can change your choice in Settings → Premium → Ad privacy choices (shown where the consent rules require it), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Notifications, alarms and the home widget never show ads. Day List Premium removes ads.

Purchases

Day List Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium and remembers the answer on your device.

Permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to deliver your reminders on time.
  • Location (only if you create location reminders): to notify you when you arrive at or leave a place. Processed on the device.
  • Photos (only when you pick one): for event header images, wish-list photos and share card backgrounds. Picked photos are copied into the app's own storage on your device.
  • Camera (only when you take a photo): to photograph shopping list items. Photos stay in the app's storage on your device.

Children

Day List is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

Because your content is stored only on your device, you control it completely: you can edit or delete anything in the app, export a backup, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. We hold no copy to delete.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes, and change the effective date above.
